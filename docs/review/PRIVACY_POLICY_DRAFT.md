## How the showroom uses your information

When you enquire about a piece, discuss a custom design or request a showroom visit, we ask for your name, phone number and permission to contact you about that request. You can include your email address. We use your email address if you choose an email reply. The showroom receives the message you submit, the relevant product details and, for appointments, your preferred time, purpose and visitor count. Please leave payment information and identity documents out of the message.

The showroom owner can review requests, record follow-up notes, update their status and confirm appointment arrangements. The system records when you consented and keeps a history of actions on the request. The public status page shows a limited summary. Your contact details, message and the owner's private notes stay out of that response.

After a successful submission you receive a reference and a private receipt token. Anyone with both can check that request's status for up to 90 days from submission. Save the receipt privately before leaving the page. The service stores a hash of the token. The **Copy private receipt** action puts your receipt on your device's clipboard. The 90-day limit applies to status lookup. Business records, owner notes and backups have separate retention arrangements **[insert the owner's approved periods and handling procedure]**.

## Your browser and external services

Your wishlist stores saved product identifiers in your browser on this device. Removing a piece from your wishlist or clearing this site's local storage removes the saved selection. The owner's signed-in workspace uses a session cookie to check access. The API uses the network address associated with a request to limit repeated requests.

Catalogue photographs can be delivered by Cloudinary. When you open a WhatsApp enquiry link, WhatsApp receives the destination and the prefilled message, which may identify a piece you are viewing. You choose whether to send that message in WhatsApp.

## Optional virtual try-on

**Live camera preview.** On supported devices you can open the camera and give your browser permission to show a live overlay. Camera frames and tracking points are processed on your device. You can close the camera or revoke permission in your browser.

**AI photo preview.** When this feature is available and you choose to generate a preview, we ask for your permission to send your photo and the selected jewellery reference to Google Gemini. The showroom service validates and re-encodes the upload, records the consent version and time, and stores temporary private input and result files. A browser cookie identifies your preview session. The browser tab can remember the job identifier to check its status again.

The preview shows its expiry time. You can request deletion earlier. Expiry or deletion ends access through the showroom service. Physical file cleanup runs in the background and can be delayed while the service is offline. Usage records are retained separately for quota accounting. Files you download or share are under your control and the control of any recipients. **[Insert verified provider handling, retention and processing locations for the actual account before enabling the photo feature.]**
