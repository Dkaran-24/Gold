# Shree Ram Jewellers website terms — owner review draft

**Publication status:** Awaiting owner review. Complete the business decisions below before publishing customer terms.

## Browsing and enquiring

Use this catalogue to explore pieces, save favourites, enquire about jewellery and request a showroom visit. Some pieces show a public price. Where the page says **Price on enquiry**, contact the showroom for details. Before making a purchase decision, ask the showroom to confirm the current availability, price, specifications, measurements and applicable charges for the particular piece.

A website enquiry starts a conversation with the showroom. A custom design enquiry shares your ideas for discussion. The owner reviews the request before agreeing any design, quotation, timing or other arrangement. Please use contact details where the showroom can reach you, and include only information relevant to your request.

## Appointments and receipts

Enter your preferred date and time using the timezone shown on the form. A successful submission gives you a request receipt. Wait for a confirmed time before travelling. If the showroom proposes a different time, contact the showroom to discuss it. The owner records the confirmation or change.

Keep your reference and private token together. You can use them to check a request's status for up to 90 days. If you lose the receipt, or a submission ends without a clear result, contact the showroom to check whether it arrived before sending another request.

## Virtual previews

When available for the selected piece, a live camera overlay or an AI-generated photo can help you explore how the jewellery might look. Lighting, pose, screen colour, tracking and generated details can affect the appearance. Confirm the physical piece, actual scale and fit with the showroom. Use a photograph you have permission to submit. Review the consent information before starting photo generation.

You decide whether to download a result, share it or open a WhatsApp conversation. Check the photo and recipient before sharing. Third-party services receive the content you choose to send through them.
