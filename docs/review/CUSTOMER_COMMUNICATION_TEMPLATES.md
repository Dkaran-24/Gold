> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Operational Security & Privacy Rules**
> 1. **Zero Financial Requests:** Never ask for bank account details, UPI PINs, credit/debit card numbers, or OTPs via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
> 2. **Zero Sensitive Documents:** Never request Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards, or passport photocopies over unencrypted messaging.
> 3. **Receipt Tokens are Private:** Never ask the customer to read out or message their 64-character private receipt token. Only ask for the public **Reference** (e.g. `REF-XXXX-XXXX`).
> 4. **No Automated Message Dispatch:** The platform does not send automated emails or SMS. All customer communications are initiated manually by authorised showroom staff from the official showroom phone or email.

### Template 2.2: Bespoke / Custom Design Enquiry Follow-Up
*Use when a customer requests a custom jewellery piece or submits reference photos.*

**Channel:** WhatsApp / Phone / Email  
**Variables:** `[Customer Name]`, `[Design Concept / Type]`, `[Next Step Details]`

```text
Namaste [Customer Name],

Thank you for sharing your bespoke jewellery design ideas with Shree Ram Jewellers.

We have reviewed your request regarding your custom [Design Concept / e.g., Bridal Choker / Solitaire Ring / Kada]. Our master craftsmen would love to discuss the details, including:
1. Preferred gold purity (18K / 22K) and metal finish.
2. Target gross weight and sizing requirements.
3. Gemstone preferences and certification details.
4. Estimated crafting timeline (typically 2 to 4 weeks).

Could we arrange a short phone consultation or an in-person showroom visit this week to review sketches and gold estimations?

Please let us know what time works best for you.

Warm regards,
Shree Ram Jewellers, Wadala
[Showroom Phone]
```
